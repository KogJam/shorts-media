# The Heiress in the Apron – story so far

## Part 1 – published 2026-11-01
Emma Hart, secretly the sole heiress of the Hart Global empire, has spent three years working as a waitress at The Golden Fork to find love that isn't about money. Her boyfriend Ryan Cole – whose business school she paid for – shows up with his new fiancée Vanessa Sterling, dumps Emma in front of the whole restaurant, Vanessa throws money and pours red wine on her, and the manager fires her. Her best friend Lily Chen witnesses everything. Emma's grandfather Arthur Hart calls her home (butler Edward, limousine) – the three-year deal is over. Arthur reveals Hart Global is buying 100% of Sterling Corporation and makes Emma CEO. At the Sterling shareholders' gala, Marcus Sterling (Vanessa's father) was about to promote Ryan to vice president; Vanessa mocks Emma and Ryan calls security – then Emma is announced as the new CEO of Hart Global. Ryan drops his glass, Vanessa panics, Lily is overjoyed. Cliffhanger: Emma says her first decision as CEO concerns two people in this room. Open threads: Ryan's promotion, the viral wine video, Lily's future, Marcus's nervousness about the buyers.
